End-to-End Projects

The most ambitious projects create the opportunity to define a new kind of experience.

Trinax connects strategy, design, engineering and long-term performance to turn ambition into experiences that move people and drive results. One partner, one connected system.

Phase 01

Think

Turn assumptions into evidence.

What

The most expensive decisions in any experience are made before anything is built. Trinax feeds design and technology decisions with evidence from human-centred research, journey studies and concept testing, before and during the build.

How

Our multidisciplinary team pairs field research with data and our Return on Experience (ROX) framework, turning insight into decision-ready strategy. Research reveals what matters. Strategy determines where to act. Creative and technical decisions follow from there.

Capabilities
Human-Centred Design ResearchInterviews, ethnography, contextual inquiry
Experience & Audience ResearchJourney mapping, field studies, behavioural tracking
Brand & Engagement ResearchBrand recall, sentiment analysis, campaign studies
Concept Testing & Rapid PrototypingAR/VR pilots, interactive prototypes
Phase 02

Build

Build what users see, touch, feel, understand and remember.

What

Between a validated direction and a live experience sits everything that can go wrong. Trinax combines design, engineering and integration under one roof.

How

Experience and service design shape the journey, software engineers the systems behind it, and hardware, fabrication and integration bring it into physical space. Quality assurance, UAT and commissioning happen in-house, before the public sees anything.

Capabilities
Experience & Service DesignJourneys, blueprints, service flows
Spatial & Environmental DesignShowrooms, exhibitions, retail experiences
UI/UX & Interaction DesignKiosks, apps, digital interfaces
Web & Mobile Application Development
Custom Platform EngineeringAR/VR systems, AI-driven personalisation
Hardware Engineering, Prototyping & Fabrication
Emerging Tech InstallationsAR/VR, IoT, interactive sensors
Phase 03

Optimise

Always on, always improving.

What

Every public-facing experience carries a quiet risk: a frozen screen or an installation switched off. A live experience is an asset that must keep earning after opening day. With Trinax, both uptime and performance are protected.

How

Optimise runs as an Experience-as-a-Service model. A dedicated team handles monitoring, preventive and corrective maintenance under SLA, supported by the engineers who built each system. Content refreshes keep campaigns current, and ROX reporting turns each period's data into the next improvement. Installations delivered years ago are still running, still under care.

Capabilities
Managed ServicesMonitoring, hosting, SLA support
Preventive & Corrective Maintenance
Creative Refresh & Campaign Optimisation
Data Reporting & ROX Insights
