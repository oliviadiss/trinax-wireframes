Peanuts Smart Redemption Platform

A seamless Peanuts-themed redemption experience across Changi Airport terminals that transforms a traditional spend-and-redeem campaign into an engaging retail journey.

Client
Changi Airport Group
Launched
2026
System
TREX™ · Smart Redemption
Footprint
Terminals 2, 3 & 4
Project film · YouTube embed 16:9 · lightbox or inline player
At a glance

A rewards counter, reimagined as a retail journey.

As part of the "Ready, Sweat, Go! with Snoopy & His Siblings" campaign, Changi Airport Group engaged Trinax to design, develop and deploy a network of smart experiential redemption machines across Changi Airport Terminals 2, 3 and 4.

Visitors who qualified through their purchases were guided through an intuitive touchscreen interface, authenticated via Changi Rewards card verification, and rewarded through an automated merchandise dispensing system.

Combined with immersive Peanuts-themed environmental branding and iconic character installations, the activation transformed a standard spend-and-redeem promotion into an experiential retail journey.

/Integrated membership card scanning technology
/Physical and digital merchandise showcase integration
/Custom experiential fabrication and Peanuts-themed branding
